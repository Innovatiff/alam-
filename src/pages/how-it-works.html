Installed on site

What "installed on site" means for you

A compact, quiet server is installed in your back office. It runs your software and stores your data. Terminals, tablets and printers connect to it over your local network.

  • Keeps working without internetSales, orders received, clock-ins and stock all run locally. Online payments and the website sync when the connection returns.
  • Your data stays in your buildingCustomer, sales and staff records live on your equipment. We can add an encrypted off-site backup if you want one.
  • Automatic backups and updatesThe server backs itself up every night. Updates are prepared by us and installed outside your busy hours.
  • Secure access from outsideCheck reports from home or approve a leave request from your phone through a secure connection, if you want to.
