Features

Everything you need, nothing you have to figure out alone

Each feature is set up by us on installation day and explained to your team in plain language.

Drag-and-drop scheduling

Build the weekly rota, copy last week, and see open shifts and staff availability at a glance.

Time clock at the door

Staff clock in and out on the tablet we install. PIN or QR code, with an optional photo, so hours are accurate.

Shift notifications

Employees see their shifts on their phone and get a reminder before each one. Changes are sent instantly.

Leave and availability

Staff request time off from their phone. You approve with one tap and the schedule updates itself.

Labour cost, live

See hours and labour cost per day next to your sales, so staffing stays right.

Timesheets and payroll export

Hours, overtime and breaks are calculated automatically. Export to Excel or to your payroll provider.

Installed on site

Runs on a small server in your back office

The software runs on a compact server or PC in your business. The clock-in tablet is mounted at the entrance and connected to it. Managers use it from any computer in the building, and staff see their schedule securely from their phones.

  • Employees and rules (breaks, overtime) configured with you
